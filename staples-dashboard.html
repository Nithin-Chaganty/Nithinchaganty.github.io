Staples Technology Sales Dashboard

BUS 351: Introduction to Business Analytics  ·  NC State

Built a four-page Tableau dashboard for Staples operational managers to track technology product sales across the US from 2014–2017. The dashboard covers accessories, copiers, machines, and phones across four regions: Central, East, South, and West.

Dashboard pages

  • Home — executive summary with key metrics, KPI indicators, and top-level regional performance
  • Orders — order-level analysis including profit percentage by region and order profit KPI benchmarks
  • Customers — customer segmentation and geographic sales distribution by state
  • Sales — product-level sales breakdown, best and worst performers by region and state

Key features

  • Geographic map showing total sales and profit by state
  • KPI indicators flagging orders above and below 20% profit benchmark
  • Tree maps and highlight tables for regional sales comparison
  • Calculated field for order profit percentage by region and sub-category
  • Interactive filters allowing managers to slice by category and region

Tools

Tableau Desktop  ·  Microsoft Excel  ·  Staples Superstore dataset (2014–2017)
