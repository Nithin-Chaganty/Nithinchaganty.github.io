Open with the point. For a project write-up: what problem you were solving and why it mattered. For an essay: the idea, stated plainly, in the first two sentences.

The problem

What was broken, unknown, or worth investigating. Include the dataset, its size, and where it came from if this is a project.

Approach

Walk through what you did. Lists work well here:

  • Step one — cleaning, joining, whatever came first
  • Step two — the modeling or analysis choice, and why
  • Step three — how you validated it

Inline code looks like train_test_split(X, y). Blocks look like this:

from sklearn.ensemble import RandomForestClassifier

model = RandomForestClassifier(n_estimators=200, random_state=42)
model.fit(X_train, y_train)

Results

Lead with the number that matters. Then explain what it means in business terms — not just the metric, but the decision it would support.

Pull out a key finding or a line worth remembering.

What I'd do differently

This section makes write-ups more credible than the ones that only report wins. Name a limitation and what you'd try next.
