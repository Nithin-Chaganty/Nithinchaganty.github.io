About

Nithin Chaganty

Raleigh, NC

I grew up in Raleigh, NC, where I was part of the STEM academy in high school. That's where it started — the interest in how things work, how data tells a story, and how you can use both to actually solve something.

I went on to NC State, where I graduated with a B.S. in Business Administration with an IT concentration and a minor in Data Science. From there I'm heading into Georgia Tech's MS in Analytics program, in the Computational Data Analytics track — which is the data science side of things.

Most of my projects end up in the health and nutrition space, not by accident. I'm genuinely interested in how data can improve health outcomes — whether that's classifying food categories from nutritional data, understanding BMI patterns, or analyzing how dietary habits compound over time. That's the kind of work I want to keep doing. A healthcare data role is the goal.

Outside of that I'm in the gym most days — weightlifting and running. The same mindset applies: track the inputs, be consistent, trust the process.

I'm currently looking for roles in data analytics, data science, and solutions engineering. If the work involves health data, even better.
